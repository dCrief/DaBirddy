Shipping

Every order ships from us with tracking, to US addresses only. We usually ship within 1–2 business days, and you’ll get an email with your tracking number when it’s on the way.

  • Tracked envelope for up to 5 cards under $20 (nothing sealed).
  • Tracked package (USPS Ground Advantage) for orders of $20 and up, more than 5 cards, or any sealed product.

The exact shipping price for your cart shows in the cart before you pay. You can check on your order any time on the Track your order page.

Returns & refunds

If a card isn’t what you expected, you can return it within 14 days of receiving it. Once the card arrives back with us, we refund you. Sealed product is final sale and can’t be returned.

How to return

  1. Email us at dabirddy@gmail.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number (it starts with DB) and which cards you’re sending back.
  2. We’ll reply with the return address.
  3. Mail the cards back in the same condition you got them, packed the way they came. Use a tracked service so we can both see when it arrives.
  4. When the cards arrive and we’ve checked them, we refund the cards’ price to your original payment method. Banks usually show refunds within 5–10 business days.

Good to know

  • Return shipping is paid by you, unless we made a mistake (wrong card, or a card that doesn’t match its photos or description), in which case we cover it and refund your original shipping too.
  • Sealed product (packs, boxes, tins and other sealed items) is final sale and can’t be returned. If it arrives damaged or never arrives, we’ll still make it right.
  • Cards bought on sale, with a coupon or through an accepted offer can be returned the same way; the refund is what you paid.
  • If your order arrives damaged or never arrives, email us right away and we’ll make it right.

Privacy

We only collect what we need to sell and ship cards, and we never sell your information.

  • Checkout: payment is handled by Stripe (including Apple Pay and Google Pay). We never see or store your card number. We keep your name, email, shipping address and what you ordered so we can ship it and help with any problems.
  • Emails: we email you about your order (confirmation, shipping, a review request). If you sign up for our list or set a “notify me” alert, we also email new drops and sales; every one of those has an unsubscribe link.
  • Cart reminders: if you’re on our email list and leave cards in your cart, we may send one reminder.
  • Your browser: the site keeps your cart, saved cards and a random id in your browser. We count page and card views with that random id to see what’s popular; it doesn’t identify you. If your browser sends “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control, we don’t count your visits.
  • Shipping: your name and address go to the shipping carrier and label service to ship your order.

Want your information removed or have a question about it? Email dabirddy@gmail.com.
